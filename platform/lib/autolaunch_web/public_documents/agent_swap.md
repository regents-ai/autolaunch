
## Agent swaps

A paired agent can have its person's buy or sell of a listed token prepared for one of the person's own wallets, exactly as the token page's swap panel offers it. The site only prepares it: your person's own wallet signs and sends each step. Autolaunch never holds a key and never signs or sends anything.

| Action | WebMCP tool | Hosted MCP tool | HTTP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare a buy or sell (signed, paired) | `autolaunch_swap_prepare` | `prepare_swap` | `POST {{origin}}/api/agent/v1/tokens/{token_id}/swap/steps` |

`token_id` is the token's `id` as `autolaunch_tokens` lists it, on Base or Robinhood Chain. A token the site does not list is refused with `token_not_found` before any chain is read. Send `direction` as `buy` to spend the pool's currency on the token, or `sell` to spend the token for the pool's currency, and `amount` as how much the wallet spends, a decimal in the side it spends. An amount that is not one, is zero, has more decimal places than that side or is more than the pool takes at once is refused with `invalid_amount` before any chain is read. `protection` is how far below today's quote the trade may settle, a number from 1 to 10 percent, 6 when you leave it out, as on the page; any other is refused with `protection_out_of_range`.

The steps are read from the token's pool at the latest block exactly as the swap panel reads them: a `token_approval` step when the wallet has not let Permit2 take `amount`, a `permit2_approval` step when Permit2 does not let the router take it until after the swap's deadline, then `swap`, which accepts no less than the minimum. They are transactions on the network `chain` names with its `chain_id`, sent in order before `expires_at`, the swap's deadline fifteen minutes after they were read; after it, prepare them again. The wallet must be on that network when it sends them. The site's pages ask a wallet to switch networks; an agent's own wallet tooling has to switch, or stop, by itself.

Every answer gives `direction`, `signer`, `token` (`id` and `address`), `chain`, the `block` it was read at, `expires_at`, `steps` (each with `step`, `to`, `data` and `value`, its `data` ending with the site's Builder Code) and `facts`: what the wallet will `pay`, what today's quote says it will `receive` and the `minimum` the swap accepts, each an `amount` with its `symbol`, the `protection` applied and the `fees` already inside the quote. A wallet that holds less than `amount` is refused with `amount_above_balance`; an amount the pool cannot fill now, with `quote_unavailable`. The wallet is `signer` when you send one of your person's own wallets, otherwise the wallet they signed in with; any other is refused with `wrong_signer`. Your person needs a verified wallet now, as `autolaunch_my_positions` does; without one, or with a `signer` that is not a wallet address, the steps are refused with `wallet_required`.

A refusal keeps its HTTP status and names its reason: 422 `invalid_request`, `invalid_amount` or `protection_out_of_range` for input to fix; 401 `authentication_required`; 403 `agent_not_paired` or `wrong_signer`; 404 `token_not_found`; 409 `wallet_required`, `amount_above_balance` or `quote_unavailable` while the wallet and the pool stand as they are; and 503 `chain_unavailable` or `swap_unavailable`, worth trying again in a moment. The hosted MCP tool at `{{origin}}/mcp` takes the same inputs, each under `input`, and gives the HTTP answer's `data` as its result; a refusal is its error, with the same code and words. The steps only come back once launches are open. The regents CLI has no swap command yet.
