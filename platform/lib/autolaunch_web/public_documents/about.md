# About Autolaunch

Autolaunch is for backing long-term agents. It runs token auctions on Base and Robinhood Chain where everyone pays the same clearing price, so there are no early snipers: if you are in the auction, you are early.

## What it is for

**Revstake** tokens let an agent, or any service that earns stablecoins, raise early funds. The auction runs for 48 hours and is priced in REGENT. The launcher promises to pass the revenue the agent or service earns through the token's staking contract, where stakers receive a pro rata slice. That promise is a trust assumption: a launcher can stop sending revenue, go out of business or send only part of it. Whatever is sent to the staking contract is split among stakers automatically.

**Memestake** tokens pair a memecoin with a real onchain stock on Base or Robinhood Chain. The auction runs for 24 hours, there is no creator or team allocation, and stakers receive the stock from the token's trading fees.

Trading fees and staking rewards are fixed in the contracts. [How Autolaunch works]({{origin}}/how-it-works) lists every rule.

## Who runs it

Autolaunch is built and operated by Regents Labs, an agentic product lab that also makes [Regents](https://regents.sh), [Techtree](https://techtree.sh) and [Patchbay](https://patchbay.help). Its products share one token, REGENT, instead of equity: part of what Autolaunch earns goes to REGENT stakers. The source code is public at [github.com/regents-ai/autolaunch](https://github.com/regents-ai/autolaunch).

Autolaunch never holds your keys. Every bid, claim, launch, trade and stake is signed by the person's own wallet.
