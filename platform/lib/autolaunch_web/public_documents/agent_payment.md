## Agent payments

A paired agent can have its person's payment or sweep into a listed token's revenue split prepared for one of the person's own wallets, exactly as the token page's Make a payment card offers it. The site only prepares it: your person's own wallet signs and sends each step. Autolaunch never holds a key and never signs or sends anything.

| Action | WebMCP tool | Hosted MCP tool | HTTP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare a payment or sweep (signed, paired) | `autolaunch_payment_prepare` | `prepare_payment` | `POST {{origin}}/api/agent/v1/tokens/{token_id}/payment/steps` |

`token_id` is the token's `id` as `autolaunch_tokens` lists it, a Revstake token on Base. A token the site does not list is refused with `token_not_found` before any chain is read; one that takes no payment on this site, such as a Memestock or Robinhood Chain token, with `payment_not_offered`; one with no revenue split, treasury or payment address yet, with `payment_not_open`. Send `kind` as `pay` to pay into the split, or `sweep` to route everything already waiting at the token's payment address into it, and `asset` as `usdc`, `regent` or `token`, the token itself. A payment needs `amount`, a decimal in the asset's own units; a sweep takes none. An amount that is not one, is zero or has more decimal places than the asset is refused with `invalid_amount` before any chain is read.

The steps are read from Base exactly as the payment card reads them: an `approval` step when a payment's wallet has not let the payment address take `amount`, then `action`, the payment or the sweep. The revenue split and the payment address are checked against the launch's own record first; if either is not the launch's own, nothing is prepared and the answer is `split_mismatch`. The steps are transactions on the network `chain` names with its `chain_id`, sent in order. The wallet must be on that network when it sends them. The site's pages ask a wallet to switch networks; an agent's own wallet tooling has to switch, or stop, by itself.

Every answer gives `kind`, `asset`, `signer`, `token` (`id` and `address`), `chain`, `steps` (each with `step`, `to`, `data` and `value`, its `data` ending with the site's Builder Code) and `facts`: the `amount` paid or routed, with its `symbol`, the `receiver` it is paid to, the `payment_reference` it carries, and the `split`: what goes to the `protocol`, to the `stakers` and to the `treasury`, each an `amount` with its `symbol`. The protocol takes 2%; the stakers' and treasury's parts are as staking stands now and change with it before the step is sent. Nothing comes back to the wallet. A wallet that holds less than `amount` is refused with `amount_above_balance`; a sweep with nothing waiting, with `nothing_to_sweep`. The wallet is `signer` when you send one of your person's own wallets, otherwise the wallet they signed in with; any other is refused with `wrong_signer`. Your person needs a verified wallet now, as `autolaunch_my_positions` does; without one, or with a `signer` that is not a wallet address, the steps are refused with `wallet_required`.

A refusal keeps its HTTP status and names its reason: 422 `invalid_request` or `invalid_amount` for input to fix; 401 `authentication_required`; 403 `agent_not_paired` or `wrong_signer`; 404 `token_not_found`; 409 `wallet_required`, `payment_not_offered`, `payment_not_open`, `split_mismatch`, `amount_above_balance` or `nothing_to_sweep` while the token and the wallet stand as they are; and 503 `chain_unavailable` or `payment_unavailable`, worth trying again in a moment. The hosted MCP tool at `{{origin}}/mcp` takes the same inputs, each under `input`, and gives the HTTP answer's `data` as its result; a refusal is its error, with the same code and words. The steps only come back once launches are open. The regents CLI has no payment command yet.
