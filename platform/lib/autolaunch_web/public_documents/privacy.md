# Privacy

What Autolaunch keeps about you, and why. Autolaunch is operated by Regents Labs, Inc., and the [Regents Labs Privacy Policy](https://regents.sh/privacy) covers it in full; this page is the short version for Autolaunch.

## Reading needs nothing

Every auction, token and public page can be read without an account or a wallet, on the website and through the public API. Autolaunch runs no advertising trackers and no analytics scripts.

## Cookies and your browser

The site sets one session cookie, which keeps you signed in and protects forms from forgery. Your browser also remembers a few conveniences on your own device, such as what you last typed in search. Privy, which handles signing in, keeps its own sign-in data in your browser.

## Signing in

Signing in is optional and handled by Privy. Autolaunch then keeps your account, the wallet you signed in with, and any accounts you choose to connect, such as X. A connected account's public name is shown on the launches you create, so backers can see who made them.

## Launch drafts

A launch you are preparing is saved as a private draft, with the pictures you upload for it, so you can come back to it. Drafts are yours until you publish them.

## What becomes public

Launches, bids, claims, trades and stakes are transactions on public blockchains. They are visible to anyone, and neither Autolaunch nor anyone else can hide or delete them. A published launch's name, description, picture and creator details are public on the site and through the API.

## Keys and payments

Autolaunch never holds your private keys and never asks for them. Every transaction is signed in your own wallet.

## Hosting, requests and removal

Autolaunch runs on Fly.io. To ask what is held about you, or to have it corrected or removed, email [privacy@regents.sh](mailto:privacy@regents.sh). The [Terms of Use](https://regents.sh/terms) apply to the site.
