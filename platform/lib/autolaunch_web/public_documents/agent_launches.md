## Agent launches

A paired agent can prepare the launch of its person's saved Revstake or Memestock draft. The site only prepares the one launch step: your person's own wallet signs and sends it, and a launch cannot be undone. Autolaunch never holds a key and never signs or sends anything.

| Action | WebMCP tool | Hosted MCP tool | HTTP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare a launch (signed, paired) | `autolaunch_launch_prepare` | `prepare_launch` | `POST {{origin}}/api/agent/v1/launches/{draft}/steps` |

`draft` is the draft's `id` as `autolaunch_revstake_draft` or `autolaunch_memestock_draft` gives it. A draft that is not your person's own is refused with `launch_draft_not_found` before any chain is read. The step is built from the chain at the latest block exactly as the create page builds it: a Revstake draft's on Base, and a Memestock draft's on the chain it names, Base or Robinhood Chain. It is a transaction on the network `chain` names with its `chain_id`, and the wallet must be on that network when it sends it. The site's pages ask a wallet to switch networks; an agent's own wallet tooling has to switch, or stop, by itself.

Every answer gives `kind` (`launch`), `signer`, `draft` (`id`, and `kind`: `revstake` or `memestock`), `chain`, the `block` it was read at, `steps` (one `launch` step: `to`, `data` and `value`, its `data` ending with the site's Builder Code), `facts`: everything the launch carries, the terms it opens with and its risk, as the create page shows them before anything is signed, and `warnings`: what else to tell your person, each with a `code` and a `message`, empty when there is nothing to say. Tell your person what `facts` and `warnings` say before they sign; a warning never holds the step back. A Revstake draft whose person has linked no X, GitHub or ENS account is given `no_connections`: its auction may not appear in the gallery or list, and your person can link one under Socials on the create page, where the same warning is shown to them. Send the step promptly, and prepare it again if launches are paused or the chain changes before it is sent. The site keeps each review it prepares, so once the launch is on the chain it is listed as your person's, with the links the draft carried. A preparation stays good for 24 hours, and at most 20 unused ones are kept, for each Base draft and for your person on Robinhood Chain. Preparing the same step as your newest one gives that one again; past 20, a different one takes the place of the oldest unused one once it is 24 hours old, and until then it is refused with `too_many_preparations`.

Any wallet may be the treasury. What only your person can give stays on the create page: the acknowledgement a treasury held by a private key takes (`launch_treasury_invalid` until it is given), their confirmation of a Safe or smart contract account you chose as the treasury (`agent_treasury_unconfirmed`), and the treasury check the create page runs, a Safe's verification included (`treasury_report_missing`). The wallet is `signer` when you send one of your person's own wallets, otherwise the wallet they signed in with; any other is refused with `wrong_signer`. Your person needs a verified wallet now, as `autolaunch_my_positions` does; without one, or with a `signer` that is not a wallet address, the launch is refused with `wallet_required`.

A refusal keeps its HTTP status and names its reason: 422 `invalid_request` for input to fix; 401 `authentication_required`; 403 `agent_not_paired`, when your pairing ended while the request ran, or `wrong_signer`; 404 `launch_draft_not_found`; 409 `wallet_required`, `launch_metadata_incomplete`, `agent_treasury_unconfirmed`, `launch_treasury_invalid`, `launch_treasury_refused`, `treasury_report_missing`, `treasury_security_changed`, `treasury_configuration_drift`, `stock_invalid`, `stock_not_admitted`, `launches_paused`, `strategy_not_bound`, `stocks_unavailable` or `robinhood_unavailable` while the draft, the chain or the site stands as it is; 429 `too_many_preparations` until the oldest of 20 unused preparations is 24 hours old; and 503 `chain_unavailable` or `launch_unavailable`, worth trying again in a moment. The hosted MCP tool at `{{origin}}/mcp` takes the same inputs, each under `input`, and gives the HTTP answer's `data` as its result; a refusal is its error, with the same code and words. The step only comes back once launches are open. The regents CLI has no launch command yet.
